Your Roadmap

The Client Journey

Our streamlined 4-step consultation structure ensures maximum transparency and documentation compliance.

1

Initial Evaluation

We review your credentials, profile history, and objectives to determine the most viable visa pathway, avoiding redundant expenses.

2

Document Audit & Prep

We guide you in gathering tax sheets, educational credentials, and employment proof, structuring your application file to meet strict embassy guidelines.

3

Submission & Tracking

Our compliance officer audits the completed file prior to official portal lodgement, booking embassy bio-metrics and tracking milestones.

4

Pre-Departure Briefing

Once approved, we coordinate port-of-entry protocol briefings, settle travel bookings, and support you in finding accommodation links.
